Application Process

How to Apply

Three simple steps to begin your education at REPTEC.

1

Download & Fill

Download the application form from reptec.org and complete all required sections carefully.

2

Submit Documents

Submit your completed form along with certified copies of your academic certificates and national ID document.

3

Choose Your Mode

Select your preferred study mode — full-time, part-time, weekend, or distance learning — and start your journey.

Entry Requirements

Requirements vary by program but generally include:

  • Junior Certificate (JC)
  • BGCSE pass or equivalent
  • National ID / Passport
  • Certified academic transcripts

Support & Guidance

Our admissions team is here to help you every step of the way:

  • One-on-one admissions advice
  • Program selection guidance
  • Document preparation help
  • Financial planning support
