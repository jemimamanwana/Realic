Admissions

Join Realic’s growing community of learners today! Flexible intake schedules and multiple learning modes make it easy to fit education into your life.

How to Apply

  • Download the application form from reptec.org.
  • Submit completed forms with academic certificates and ID.
  • Choose your preferred study mode: full-time, part-time, weekend, or distance learning.

Entry Requirements

Requirements vary by program but generally include a Junior Certificate (JC) or Botswana General Certificate of Secondary Education (BGCSE) pass.

Support & Assistance

Admissions staff are available to guide you through the process. Contact us for help or advice.
