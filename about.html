About Realic College

Realic Professional & Technical College is a private, accredited institution located at African Mall, Gaborone. Established to meet Botswana’s growing need for practical, career-oriented education, Realic blends academic theory with real-world training.

Mission

To provide accessible, flexible, and high-quality education that builds skills and transforms careers.

Vision

To be Botswana’s leading provider of professional and technical education, recognized for excellence, innovation, and integrity.

Accreditation

All programs are accredited by the Botswana Qualifications Authority (BQA).
